4. **R19.S1 check 1 (spec R19.S1, §5.1 "Ask one question per message").**
   The check now counts questions, not `?` characters, in the same text as
   before: outside code, quotes and fenced blocks.
   - The text is cut into paragraphs, with each list item a paragraph of its
     own, and then into sentences.
   - A sentence holding a `?` opens a question when it is the first such
     sentence, or when it opens with a question word. The question words
     are what, which, where, when, who, why and how, and verbs that lead a
     question, such as is, do, can, should and would. The opening word is
     read after any list marker, emphasis, short label ending in a colon
     (`My question:`) and joining word (and, also, then, next, second).
   - Any other sentence holding a `?` is part of the question before it.
     That covers examples (`For example: ..., or something else?`),
     choices (`Or should alpha read it?`) and an options list.
